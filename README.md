🌉 About RareBridge

RareBridge is a digital platform designed to help individuals and families better understand rare diseases through accessible, structured, and trustworthy health information.

Rare diseases can be difficult to understand and often come with limited accessible information. RareBridge aims to bridge this knowledge gap by bringing disease information, diagnosis guidance, treatment and research information, specialist resources, and community support into one platform.

The platform is designed to make complex medical information clearer, more organized, and easier to navigate, while maintaining a professional healthcare experience.
